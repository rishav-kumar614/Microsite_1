About

Coaching Grounded in
Lived Leadership

Dr. Krishan Singh is an Executive Coach, Leadership Mentor, and ICF Associate Certified Coach (ACC) with over 30 years of enterprise leadership experience. His coaching is distinguished by the depth of what he has personally seen, led, and lived — from boardroom strategy to plant-floor operations, across four countries.

As Senior Vice President – HR at SRF Limited, he led people strategy and organisational transformation across multi-plant manufacturing units, R&D centres, industrial relations, and international operations. This experience directly informs every coaching conversation he has today.
